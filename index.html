About

I am a Research Assistant at EPFL, working with Prof. Martin Jaggi. I hold an MSc from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), where my advisor was Prof. Petros Maragos, and completed my master's thesis at EPFL under Prof. Jaggi's supervision.

I work on making AI models more robust, exploratory, and adaptable. I'm particularly interested in how models align with our goals as they learn from each other and from their own outputs, and in the training dynamics that govern when models memorize, reason, or generalize.
