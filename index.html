About

I am currently a Research Assistant at EPFL. I completed my master's degree at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA). I conducted my master's thesis at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Martin Jaggi. My advisor at NTUA was Prof. Petros Maragos.

I work on making AI models more steerable and capable of reliable reasoning, focusing on generalization and robustness under distribution shifts. I'm particularly interested in how reasoning fails through hallucinations or faulty steps, and how we can guide training to learn representations aligned with our goals. I see promise in training-dynamics-aware approaches that intelligently select which samples and tokens to train on, with scientific inference and healthcare applications as motivating testbeds.
