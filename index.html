About

I am a master's student at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA). I am currently conducting my master's thesis at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Martin Jaggi. My advisor at NTUA is Prof. Petros Maragos.

As my undergraduate studies are coming to an end, I am keen to continue my research as a PhD student. Having experienced working in an academic research environment, I am confident that such a setting is ideal for me. I am broadly interested in improving the generalization ability and robustness of AI models, making them as reliable and safe as they are capable. I want to understand how and when models fail to generalize, how issues such as hallucinations, inconsistent reasoning, or unstable behavior are provoked when models are deployed in real-world, potentially adversarial, settings.

I would like to draw on both theoretical insights and evidence from these failures to improve training—whether in pretraining, finetuning, or alignment—by developing objectives and signals that enhance reasoning and reliability. I see exciting opportunities to extend these directions to multimodal models and agents, where the combination of perception, reasoning, and action presents additional challenges. I am particularly motivated by high-stakes domains such as medicine, where advances in reasoning and reliability could directly improve human health. My broader aim is to reduce failure modes and contribute to more trustworthy AI systems.
