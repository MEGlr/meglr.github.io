About Me

Hello! I am Maria, a master's student at EPFL specializing in Artificial Intelligence. My academic journey started at NTUA, where I developed a strong foundation in computer science and a passion for research.

With experience in AI, vision systems, and teaching, I aim to contribute to advancements in AI generalization and robustness. Outside of academics, I enjoy traveling and exploring different cultures.

Research Interests

My research focuses on improving the generalization and robustness of AI models. Key interests include:
